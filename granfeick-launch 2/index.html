Parent company

GranFeick LLC is the home base for focused, durable products.

GranFeick LLC is a place to build with care, patience, and intention in the details. We are starting with personal finance because the best software does more than store numbers. It should reduce friction, make tradeoffs visible, and leave people feeling steadier when they make a plan.

First product

Clocktown turns budgeting into a timeline.

Clocktown is being designed for households that need to see what is coming: paydays, bills, subscriptions, spending categories, and savings goals in one time-aware view.
