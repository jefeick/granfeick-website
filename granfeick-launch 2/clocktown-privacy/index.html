Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

GranFeick LLC respects your privacy. This website does not collect personal information unless you choose to contact us, request support, or send feedback.

Clocktown Budgeting is still in development. If future app features involve accounts, bank connections, transactions, or other financial data, this policy will be updated before those features are publicly offered.

For privacy questions, contact us at support@granfeick.com.
